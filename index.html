Introduction

About Me

I am a Software Engineer with over five years of professional experience designing and building enterprise-grade backend systems. Based in Abuja, I currently lead software development at Hasob Integrated Services, where I architect multi-tenant platforms serving government agencies and large institutions.

My core expertise spans the PHP/Laravel and Node.js ecosystems, with deep practical experience in relational and document databases, containerised deployments, and RESTful API design. I approach engineering with an emphasis on system reliability, maintainability, and long-term scalability.

I am pursuing an M.Sc. in Information Management at ABU Zaria, where my research sits at the intersection of software engineering and knowledge systems. Open to remote engagements with teams building meaningful, high-impact software.
